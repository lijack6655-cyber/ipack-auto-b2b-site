Meet I-PACK

Japanese vehicle parts.
A practical B2B focus.

I-PACK supplies aftermarket auto lights and body parts from Guangzhou, working with importers, wholesalers and distributors.

Our product range includes headlights, tail lights, fog lights, mirror covers, bumper parts and related exterior components for Japanese vehicles. A vehicle model, OE number or product photo gives our team a starting point for matching and quotation.

Whether you are building a mixed order or discussing repeat supply, the details come first: part specification, quantity, packing and destination.
