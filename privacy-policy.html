Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Who controls your data

Guangzhou I-Pack Auto Parts Co., Limited operates this website. Privacy requests can be sent to lynda.yang@ipackauto.com.

Data we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we may collect your name, business contact details, company, country, vehicle and part requirements, OE number, quantity, message, submitted images, source page and campaign parameters. We also create a one-way request fingerprint for spam and duplicate prevention; we do not store the raw IP address in the inquiry record.

Why we use it

We use this information to answer quotation and fitment requests, provide requested B2B services, prevent abuse, maintain transaction records and improve inquiry handling. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers and international transfers

Authorized hosting, database, form-delivery and communication providers may process data for us. Data may be processed outside your country with reasonable contractual and technical safeguards.

Retention and security

Inquiry records are scheduled for review or deletion after 24 months unless a longer period is needed for an active business relationship, legal obligation or dispute. Access is role-restricted and protected through authenticated systems and database security policies.

Your rights

Depending on your location, including the EEA, UK or California, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or information about processing, and may object or withdraw consent where applicable. Email us to exercise these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies and analytics

The current site uses essential browser storage for the inquiry list. Optional analytics will only be activated with an appropriate consent mechanism where required.

Updates

We may update this policy when our systems or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
