## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing static HTML/CSS/JavaScript website with Vercel API routes. Extend the existing implementation; no framework migration is requested.

## Users

International B2B buyers exploring Japanese-vehicle aftermarket auto lights and body parts, then requesting fitment confirmation and a quotation. Our Brand serves visitors who want to understand the company before making an inquiry.

## Product Purpose

Connect published product discovery, vehicle/OE search and RFQ. The user requests a concise product-led homepage and a separate Our Brand destination for company information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product is the product showcase; Search is the vehicle/OE/RFQ center.
- Published catalog and categories are supplied through existing backend endpoints. Do not duplicate or alter product ownership for visual work.
- Existing inquiry lists, contact details, consent, form fields and photo handling must remain functional.
- The current task authorizes design decisions and implementation of floating contact controls, a homepage company introduction and Our Brand. Preserve the existing About URL.
- Production publication is distinct from preview validation and visual acceptance.

## Brand Commitments

I-PACK identity and existing logo. User references Luxopack for floating contact controls and video presentation, Autoparts6 for automotive merchandising, and Kavnar for clean product presentation. Preserve the established site identity when extending it.
